## 👥 Friendr
### Consent-First Social Connection

A free, ad-free social app built around three opt-in connection layers:

- **Friends** — platonic hangouts, local groups, and community feeds
- **A Bit More** — spontaneous proximity matching when you're available
- **Way More** — intentional deeper connections with mutual escalation and pace guardrails

Every account is 18+ ID-verified with human moderation. No bots, no ads, no data sold.

**Website:** https://friendr-web.vercel.app

## 🌿 StrainVerse
### Stoner-First Social Network

A cannabis-centered social platform built around strain culture and community conversations.

- StrainVerse directory with strain pages, reviews, and photos
- HerbHub, SocialSesh group chats, and MatchIt connections
- Profile "stash" pages and community feeds
- Powered by Supabase and Gemini AI

**Website:** https://strainverse.vercel.app

## 🥃 SpiritsVerse
### The Social Network for Drink Lovers

- Location-aware pub and bar experiences

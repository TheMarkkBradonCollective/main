Our story

From skating rink to cultural room

The Rink Studios is a creative event venue and community hub in a historic brownstone that once housed a beloved local skating rink. The wheels have stopped. The instinct to gather did not.

Today the floor holds live music, fashion shows, private events, brand activations, and cultural nights — built so Sacramento artists, curators, and planners have a room where the production matches the idea.

More than a rental, The Rink is a landmark on Del Paso Blvd: staff, regulars, and first-timers sharing the same brick shell. If you are performing, hosting, or just showing up, you are in that line.

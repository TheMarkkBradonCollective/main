Our story

The wheels stopped. The room did not.

The Rink Studios sits in a classic brownstone that once held a beloved local skating rink. The floor still wants people on it — now for bands, runways, brand activations, and the nights neighbors actually remember.

Artists, curators, and planners use the space because the production is real: modular stage, concert sound, cameras, a VIP balcony, and a load-in door that does not make a crew invent a miracle.

Front Page / About

Markeith Nicholas White

Markk Brandon · StickzCoder.Lua

I'm the developer behind The Markk Brandon Collective — full-stack websites and apps built out of Sacramento. Late nights, real backends, no “book a demo” nonsense. Some of these started because I got sick of apps charging for basics that should be free — those stay free to use, no matter what. Others — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — I made because I was bored, curious, or had a random idea at 1 a.m. and couldn't let it go.

Before the web apps I ran game servers for five years — Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yes, a Roblox server. Communities don't stick around for a pretty landing page; they stick around when stuff works. That's the energy I brought here.

To pull this off I write and ship in Cursor, track everything on GitHub, run the backends through Supabase, host on Vercel, and point domains with GoDaddy. AI speeds up the typing. I still own the architecture, the deploys, and the 2 a.m. fix when something breaks.

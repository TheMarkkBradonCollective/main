Friendr

Friends. A bit more. Way more.

Listing #003 ● Active Social & Connection

Friendr is a free, ad-free social app built around consent at every step. Three opt-in connection layers let you choose exactly how far things go — and others only see you in sections you've joined.

  • Friends — platonic hangouts, local groups, community feeds
  • A Bit More — spontaneous proximity matching when you're available
  • Way More — intentional deeper connections with mutual escalation
  • 18+ ID verification with human moderation on every account
  • No bots, no ads, no data sold — free forever
  • iOS & Android apps coming soon

Community

Free on purpose

Why this exists

Sacramento is home. I watched good stuff hit the curb while neighbors needed the same things — and every “free” app still wanted a cut somewhere. So I built something that stays free: give, ask, no selling, no flipping, no ads. The Android app adds live Go Get pickup.

Lifestyle & Culture

Boredom / fun / random ideas

Why this exists

StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse, and GigOS weren't born from a pitch deck. Created solely out of boredom, for fun, or off the top of a random idea. Culture stuff I wanted to play with — cannabis, drinks, cooking, live shows — turned into real apps with real databases, not throwaway demos. Still free to use. Still meant to get used.

Social & Connection

Spite for paywalled apps

Why this exists

A lot of social apps weren't built for you — they were built for advertisers. Bots, fake profiles, data sold off, paywalls for basics. Friendr, Chatr, and Findr are my answer: consent first, free to use, real databases, human moderation where it matters. Check them out. If they help, help keep them online.

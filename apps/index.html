Social & Connection

Spite for paywalled apps

Why this exists

A lot of social apps weren't built for you — they were built for advertisers. Bots, fake profiles, data sold off, paywalls for basics. Friendr, Findr, and Chatr are my answer: consent first, free to use, real databases, human moderation where it matters. Check them out. If they help, help keep them online.

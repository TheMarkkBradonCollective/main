Welcome to The Markk Brandon Collective — the classifieds for everything I've been building out of Sacramento. Late nights. Real backends. No “book a demo” nonsense. Some of these started because I got sick of apps charging for basics that should be free — those stay free to use, no matter what. Others — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — I made because I was bored, curious, or had a random idea at 1 a.m. and couldn't let it go. All of them are here for you to try on your own: live on Vercel, data in Supabase, source on GitHub, domains on GoDaddy, built in Cursor when I need to move.

Before all this I ran game servers for five years — Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yes, a Roblox server. Communities don't stick around for a pretty landing page; they stick around when stuff works. That's the energy I brought to the web apps.

Three doors on this site: The Classifieds — browse and try the work. GoFundMe — help keep the free ones online if something actually helped you. Hire Me — want your own site or app? Tell me what you're picturing and your budget. I'll reach out if it's a fit.

Real talk, up front

I use AI. Not hiding it. You've seen the ads — one prompt, boom, you've got an “app.” What they don't tell you is how basic that actually is. A front page. Maybe a button that goes nowhere. Boring. Dead on arrival. That is not what I built. That is not what I showcase here.

What's actually here is full-stack websites and apps — complete backends, not brochureware. Logins that work. Data that persists. Permissions that mean something. APIs behind every screen. Android builds you can install on your phone. Tap something — something happens. Products, not previews.

To pull this off I write and ship in Cursor — that's where the actual building happens, feature by feature, bug by bug. Every change goes to GitHub so nothing vanishes into the void; I can see what shipped, when, and roll back when I break something at midnight. Supabase is the backend: real Postgres tables, auth so people can sign in, storage for uploads and assets, row-level security so users only see what's theirs. Vercel keeps the sites and apps live — push to main and it's up, no server closet to babysit. GoDaddy holds the domains and DNS so guardr.co, signaturesecurityspecialist.com, and the rest actually resolve to the right deploy instead of a parking page. AI speeds up the typing. It doesn't replace knowing how any of this fits together. I still own the architecture, the deploys, and the 2 a.m. fix when something breaks.

Stack that against what those ads are selling and you're looking at roughly 67 🫴 6 7 🫴 😏% more than a one-prompt landing page. Open The Classifieds. Sideload an APK if you want it on your phone. Click into something that interests you, use it for real, and see if any of it actually helps — that's what all of this is here for.

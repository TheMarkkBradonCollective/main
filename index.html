Real talk, up front

Yes — I use AI to build. Cursor, Vercel, Supabase, the whole stack. I'm not hiding that. The commercials are right: you can spit out an “app” in seconds with one prompt. That is not this.

Without the knowledge I already had in code, servers, and shipping real stuff, I couldn't finish these as full-stack products — not just a pretty frontend, not a half-built page with buttons that don't click. I work them part by part, over a long time. I test them. I make sure everything is functional. And most of all, they run to a real database — so these sites do more than display. Call it roughly 67% more functionality than your average front page. Yeah — 🫴 6 7 🫴 😏

I go way deeper than a one-shot preview. I create something usable. I create an experience. And I don't stop until the project is done — and in my eyes, no project is ever really “done.”

This is The Markk Brandon Collective — a group of projects I built, all under one roof, for people to check out on their own. Cursor. Vercel. Supabase. Late nights. Some of them came out of spite for apps that won't stop charging users for basics. My goal on those is simple: keep them free to use, no matter what. Others — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — came purely out of boredom, for fun, or off the top of a random idea. Either way, I want to see them get used. So I'm listing them here as a showcase, and I'm operating them under the Markk Brandon Collective.

Before the websites and apps, I spent about five years building game servers — Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yes, sadly, a Roblox server. The last two to three years I've been deep in full websites and apps. That path is why I can ship more than a landing page.

This site does three jobs: showcase the work so you can try it, GoFundMe so the free apps can keep running (because free for you still costs me), and an open door to request a build if you want me to make something for you — with your price range, so I can reach out.

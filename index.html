Guardr

Independent Security Marketplace

An independent security marketplace connecting people with protection services — anytime, anywhere. Built as a platform for discovering and engaging security professionals on demand.

Visit guardr.co

Friendr

Consent-First Social Connection

A free, ad-free social app built around three opt-in connection layers: Friends (platonic hangouts and local groups), A Bit More (spontaneous proximity matching), and Way More (intentional deeper connections with mutual escalation). Every account is 18+ ID-verified with human moderation — no bots, no ads, no data sold.

Visit friendr-web.vercel.app
🌿

StrainVerse

Stoner-First Social Network

A cannabis-centered social platform built around strain culture and community. Browse the StrainVerse directory, explore HerbHub, join SocialSesh group chats, use MatchIt for connections, and curate your profile stash — powered by Supabase and Gemini AI.

  • Strain pages, reviews, and photos
  • Community feeds and group sessions
  • Age-gated features with geolocation
Visit strainverse.vercel.app
🥃

SpiritsVerse

The Social Network for Drink Lovers

A social platform for cocktails, beers, spirits, and wine. Explore the Drink Directory, scroll SipStream, find your Local Pub, join BarSesh groups, and share your digital bar with drinking buddies — with Gemini-powered features like drunken wisdom and bar-lens imagery.

  • Drink profiles, reviews, and tasting notes
  • PourUp social feed and community stories
  • Location-aware pub discovery
Visit spiritsverse.vercel.app
🍳

Cookbook.io

The Social Table

A social cooking platform featuring Chef Gemini, an AI kitchen concierge that helps with flavor pairings, gourmet substitutions, cooking techniques, and recipe guidance. More than a recipe box — it's a shared table for kitchen experiments and culinary discovery.

Visit cookbook-io-ten.vercel.app
🤝

Sacramento Buy Nothing

Give Freely. Ask Kindly.

A community-powered free gifting platform for Sacramento neighbors. Post items you no longer need, request what you're looking for, and connect locally — with no selling, no bidding, and no flipping. 100% free, no ads, ever. Built with Supabase and Vercel.

Visit sacramentobuynothing.com
🔒

Signature Security Specialist

Protect with Purpose · Perform with Excellence

An executive-level security company connecting qualified guards, event staff, and medical responders with clients across Sacramento and the San Francisco Bay Area. Features a mobile-first operations platform with mission scheduling, live tracking, a 10-rank career system, certifications, training (LMS), and role-based dashboards for guards, staff, and clients.

  • Unarmed, armed, event, retail, and mobile patrol
  • SigSecSpec Companion & Messenger apps
  • Currently obtaining licenses — applications accepted
Visit signaturesecurityspecialist.com

This is The Markk Brandon Collective — a showcase of projects I built with Cursor, Vercel, Supabase, and a whole lot of late nights. Some of them came out of spite for apps that keep charging people for basic features. My goal there is simple: keep them free to use, no matter what. Others — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — came from boredom, for fun, or straight off a random idea. Either way, I want to see them actually get used. So I'm listing them here, under one umbrella, for people to check out on their own.

Before all this web and app work, I spent about five years building game servers — Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yeah, sadly, even a Roblox server. The last two to three years I've been deep in full websites and apps. I feel ready to put myself out there and build for other people too — for a fee, of course. If you want something made, there's a request form for that.

Keeping free apps online isn't free for me. Hosting, databases, domains, tools — it adds up. If you like what you see and want this stuff to keep running the way it should, there's a GoFundMe page so you can chip in.

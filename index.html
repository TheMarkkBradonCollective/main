Real talk, up front

Yes — I use AI to build. I'm not hiding it. The ads are right: you can generate a fake “app” in one prompt. That is not this.

I code in Cursor, deploy on Vercel, run real databases on Supabase, keep source on GitHub, and register domains through GoDaddy. The stack speeds me up — it doesn't replace knowing how auth, APIs, and production deploys actually work. I build feature by feature, test what ships, and wire every project to a live backend so it does something.

These aren't brochure sites. Roughly 67 🫴 6 7 🫴 😏% more functionality than your average front page — logins, saved data, stuff that persists when you close the tab.

I ship things people can actually use. And in my eyes, no project is ever really done.

The Markk Brandon Collective is every app and site I've built — listed here so you can try them on your own. Some started because other apps wouldn't stop charging for basics; those stay free to use, no matter what. Others — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — came from boredom, a random idea, or just wanting to see if I could pull it off. Either way they're real products: hosted on Vercel, backed by Supabase, tracked in GitHub, on domains I own through GoDaddy, built in Cursor when I need the speed.

Before the web apps I spent five years running game servers — Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yes, a Roblox server. The last two to three years I've been shipping full websites and Android apps. That's the difference between a landing page and something that saves your data.

This site does three things: The Classifieds so you can browse and try the work, GoFundMe because free for you still costs me to host, and Hire Me if you want something built — tell me what you need and your budget, and I'll reach out if it's a fit.

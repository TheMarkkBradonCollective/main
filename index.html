Community

Section I

Why This Section Exists

Sacramento is my home. I watched neighbors throw away perfectly good furniture, clothes, and tools while other neighbors were struggling to afford the same things — and every existing platform wanted money in the middle. Buy/sell groups turn gifting into commerce. Marketplace apps invite flipping and bidding. Nobody was building something that was simply free, local, and human.

Sacramento Buy Nothing is the community venture group — mutual aid without a price tag. Give freely, ask kindly, keep good things out of the landfill, and let neighbors help neighbors with no ads and no paywalls, ever.

Social & Connection

Section III

Why This Section Exists

I started building in social because the apps people use every day weren't built for them — they were built for advertisers. Bots, fake profiles, data sold without consent, and algorithms that push engagement over honesty. I wanted something different: a place where real people connect on their own terms, where every layer of intimacy is opt-in, and where safety isn't a marketing line but the foundation of the product.

Friendr is the answer to that problem. It's the social venture group — software where consent comes first, moderation is human, and the platform is free because your attention isn't the product.

Security & Protection

Section IV

Why This Section Exists

Protection is the thread that runs through everything I do — it's in the motto: Code. Create. Protect. Share. Repeat. I didn't stumble into security; I built a career around it. Signature Security Specialist started because professional security operations in Northern California needed a company that treated guards like customer-service professionals, not brute force. Guardr started because people deserve access to protection on their own terms — an independent marketplace, not a gatekept industry.

This is the security venture group — the businesses and platforms built to protect persons, places, and things, backed by mobile-first technology, training systems, and the belief that real security is professionalism first, always.

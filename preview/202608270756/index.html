Quick answers

FAQ

Do I need to bring my own skates?

No. Quad rentals are $5 and rollerblades are $7. Bring your own if you want.

How much is admission?

Most public sessions are $12. Tuesday coffee club is $8 ($10 with rental). Extra sessions $5 Fri/Sat.

What ages can skate?

All ages for public sessions. Adult nights are 18+.

Do you offer birthday parties?

Yes — private 2- and 3-hour packages. Public-session birthday packages are paused; private parties are still booking.

Can I bring outside food?

Yes for private parties. No alcohol. Bring your own plates, napkins, cups, and utensils. Snack bar is open for purchase.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. Socks required for rentals.

Do I need to reserve ahead?

Walk-ins welcome for public skate. Parties and fundraisers need a call or request during office hours.

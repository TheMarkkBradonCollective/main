Front Page / Hire Me

This page stays open for people who want me to build their website or app. I've gained a ton of knowledge developing these projects over the past two to three years — right after about five years building game servers (Minecraft, FiveM, Assetto Corsa, Beaming, and yes, sadly, a Roblox server). That path taught me how to ship things people actually use, not just screenshots.

I feel ready to put myself out there and start building for others — at a cost, of course. Here's where you make that request: say what you want, add your price range (min–max), and I'll reach out if it's something I can take on. No fake “instant quote” bots. Just me reading what you sent.

What you get when we work together is the same standard I hold my own apps to — full stack when that's what you need, real databases, tested flows, buttons that work. AI helps me move faster; the experience is what makes it finish. And I don't call a project done until it feels usable.

Want to support the free apps instead of hiring? That's the Support / GoFundMe page — different door, same Collective.

How it works

Simple process

  1. You fill out the form — what you need, who it's for, must-haves, and your budget min–max.
  2. I read it — if the scope and budget can meet, I'll email you back.
  3. We talk it through — goals, timeline, what's in / out, how we'll build it.
  4. I build and ship — part by part, tested, connected to whatever backend you need — not a dead preview.

Submits to my inbox. I'll reply if it's something I can take on. Be honest on the budget — it saves both of us time.

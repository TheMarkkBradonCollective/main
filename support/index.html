Keep My Free Apps Online — The Markk Brandon Collective

Suggested story (paste into GoFundMe)

I'm Markk Brandon — developer behind The Markk Brandon Collective. Over the past couple years I've shipped a pile of real apps and websites that are free to use on purpose: no ads, no data selling, no nickel-and-diming you for basics.

There's Friendr (consent-first social), Findr (location for people you trust), Chatr (encrypted sticky-note chat), Sacramento Buy Nothing (neighborhood gifting), and the Verse family — StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, and Cookverse. I'm also building Guardr, an independent security marketplace, and Signature Security Specialist, my security company. Free apps live on The Classifieds; the security desk is the Security Showcase.

Here's the honest part: free for users isn't free for me. Every app needs hosting, databases (Supabase), domains, email, SSL, and ongoing maintenance. Bugs don't fix themselves. Servers don't pay themselves. I built these because I wanted tools that respect people — and I want to keep them running instead of shutting them down when a bill lands.

What your donation covers:

  • Server and database costs so apps stay online 24/7
  • Domain renewals and email for account flows
  • Bug fixes, security patches, and feature updates
  • Time to keep shipping — not to run ads or sell your data

I'm not asking you to fund a startup pitch. I'm asking you to help keep something useful alive. If you've tried any of these apps and they helped you — or if you just believe free, privacy-respecting software should exist — I'd be grateful for any amount.

If you want me to build your project, that's a separate conversation — the Hire Me form on the site. This fundraiser is only for keeping the free Collective apps operating.

Thank you for reading. Thank you for using the work. And thank you if you can chip in.

— Markk Brandon (Markeith Nicholas White)

Plain English — no fluff

Keeping free apps free

Friendr, Findr, Chatr, Buy Nothing, StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — they're meant to stay free. Support covers the boring necessary stuff: servers, databases, domains, email, monitoring. The bill doesn't vanish because the product is free for you.

Shipping and fixing

Bugs. Updates. New features. Making sure buttons work and data saves. These aren't preview sites — they're full apps that need ongoing care. In my eyes no project is ever really “done,” and that work has a cost.

Getting them used

I didn't build all this to sit in a folder. I want people checking them out, poking around, actually using them. Support helps me keep that door open instead of taking things offline when costs stack up.

Not the hire-me lane

Donating here supports the free umbrella apps. If you want me to build your website or app, that's a different door — the Hire Me request form, with your budget range.

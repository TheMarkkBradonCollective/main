Keep it running

Free apps still cost money to operate

Front Page / Support

I've created a crap ton of apps that are all free to use — and that costs me. Hosting, databases, domains, tools, time. I want to keep these going and get them used, but to do that they have to keep operating the way they should. That bill doesn't vanish just because the product is free for you.

This page is the GoFundMe lane. Check out the showcase, try the stuff that interests you, and if you want these free apps to stay alive and keep improving, chip in. Every bit helps keep the lights on and the databases warm.

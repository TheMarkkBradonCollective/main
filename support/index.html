Keep it running

Free for you still costs me — help keep the apps operating

Front Page / Support

I've created a crap ton of apps that are all free to use — and that costs me. Hosting. Databases. Domains. Tools. Time. I want to keep these going and get them used, but to do that I need to keep them operating the way they should. That costs money. Continuously.

This page is the GoFundMe lane. The idea is simple: you check out The Classifieds, try the stuff that interests you on your own, and if you want these free apps to stay alive and keep improving, you can donate. I'm not selling your data. I'm asking for help keeping the lights on so the free stuff stays free.

Every bit helps — servers stay up, Supabase stays warm, domains don't expire, and I can keep fixing and shipping instead of shutting things down when the bill hits.

Where support goes

What your donation is actually for

Plain English — no fluff

Keeping free apps free

Friendr, Findr, Chatr, Buy Nothing, StrainVerse, SpiritsVerse, Cookverse — they're meant to stay free. Support covers the boring necessary stuff: servers, databases, domains, email, monitoring. The bill doesn't vanish because the product is free for you.

Shipping and fixing

Bugs. Updates. New features. Making sure buttons work and data saves. These aren't preview sites — they're full apps that need ongoing care. In my eyes no project is ever really “done,” and that work has a cost.

Getting them used

I didn't build all this to sit in a folder. I want people checking them out, poking around, actually using them. Support helps me keep that door open instead of taking things offline when costs stack up.

Not the hire-me lane

Donating here supports the free umbrella apps. If you want me to build your website or app, that's a different door — the Hire Me request form, with your budget range.
